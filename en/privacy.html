2. What data is processed

Coach Planner stores all content exclusively on your device. The app has no user account and no server of its own; your content is not transmitted to the Provider or to third parties. Stored locally are:

  • Usage content: exercises, training plans, training groups, training sessions and circles you create, including free-text fields such as notes, titles and training goals
  • Content other coaches have shared with you and that you import
  • App settings, e.g. the name the app greets you with, if you enter one, and whether you have accepted an amended version of the terms of use

The Provider has no access to this data.

3. Purposes of processing

The locally stored data serves solely to provide the app's features on your device.

4. Legal basis

Insofar as the Provider processes any data, this is based on Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of the usage agreement between you and the Provider).

5. Recipients

The Provider uses no processors. The app does not pass any data to third parties and contains no analytics, tracking or advertising services.

If you download the app from the Apple App Store or Google Play, the respective store operator processes data under its own responsibility; its privacy policy applies.

If you create a backup or a PDF export, or share exercises and trainings with other coaches, you decide who receives the file.

6. Retention period

The data stays on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. The Provider cannot restore deleted data. Protect yourself against data loss with a backup (Settings → Data → "Create backup").

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access to the data stored about you (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Erasure of your data (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Objection to processing (Art. 21 GDPR)
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority

As the Provider stores no data about you, you exercise these rights directly in the app: you can view, change and delete your content there at any time. Settings → Data → "Create backup" gives you your data in a structured, machine-readable format (JSON).

Contact for rights requests
coachapp.info@gmail.com

8. Minors and third parties

The Provider deliberately holds no data on players or other third parties. Entering personal data of third parties into the free-text fields is prohibited under section 5 of the terms of use — use neutral labels instead of names. If you enter such data despite that prohibition, you are responsible for it.

Use of the app requires a minimum age of 16. For minors, what is set out in section 3 of the terms of use applies in addition.

9. Data security

Your content only leaves your device if you pass on a file yourself — a backup, a PDF export or shared exercises and trainings. Anyone with access to your unlocked device can see the app's content — protect it with a screen lock.
